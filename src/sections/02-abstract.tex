\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

Battery management systems (BMS) are verified on hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) rigs that generate large volumes of sensor data.
At Analog Devices, the industry partner, these data are accessed through raw logs and offline scripts, so anomalies are often identified only after a run has ended and results are difficult to communicate to project leads and customers.

This project proposes a read-only monitoring dashboard, designed as a digital shadow of the test rig, and uses it to investigate four questions: whether data can be streamed from the rig in near real time without affecting it; which filtering methods suppress noise without concealing genuine faults; whether alarm-handling rules can reduce the number of alerts without missing critical events; and whether role-specific views reduce task time for each audience.
The work follows a Design Science Research approach and is evaluated without human participants, using recorded and simulated data containing faults introduced at known times.

At this stage, the requirements analysis, literature review, system architecture and experimental design are complete.
Implementation will begin after this interim checkpoint.
