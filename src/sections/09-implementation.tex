The literature review influenced the project in three respects.
First, it narrowed the research gap: dashboards with alerting already exist for test benches, but they have not been evaluated.
Second, the evidence on chattering alarms~\cite{wang2016alarmoverview} established hysteresis and deduplication as core features of the alert engine.
Third, the limited coverage of automated accessibility tools~\cite{vigo2013benchmarking} led to the addition of a manual checklist.

The requirements analysis translated the goals of the brief into 19 testable requirements and revealed how much of the partner's plan was company work rather than research (Table~\ref{tab:deliverables}).
