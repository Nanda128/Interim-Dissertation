\textbf{Portfolio alternative.} The RQs split cleanly into separate papers, so the work also suits a portfolio of IEEE-format papers with a 2--4 page introductory chapter linking them: (A) the non-intrusive streaming architecture and its benchmark (RQ1); (B) signal conditioning and alarm management for BMS test KPIs (RQ2, RQ3); and (C) analytical evaluation of role-adaptive views (RQ4).
Paper~C is the weakest on its own without a user study, so a two-paper version that folds RQ4 into Paper~A is also possible.
